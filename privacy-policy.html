This privacy statement governs the collection and use of personal data of visitors and clients on the HostZin platform. Our static website has been designed to maximize user privacy and limit data exposure.

1. Data Collection and Processing

We believe in absolute data minimization. Because our public website is compiled as standard static HTML files without active third-party tracking scripts, cookie managers, or analytics tracking suites, we process very little of your personal information:

  • Contact Form Data: When you submit a pre-sales message through our public Contact Us form, the name, email, and message content you provide are processed temporarily on our server to send an automated notification. This information is never stored or used for secondary marketing lists.
  • Web Server Log Files: Like all professional hosting environments, our servers write standard, anonymous technical logs (IP addresses, web browser strings, and files accessed) to ensure platform security, stability, and protection against malicious DDoS attacks.

2. Use of Cookies

Our public-facing site does not load or place commercial advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party marketing elements. We only write temporary, functional system sessions when necessary for form submission security and styling preferences.

3. Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or lease your private personal details to third-party commercial marketing firms. When you click our pricing call-to-action buttons, you are transferred to LTHost's secure checkout environment, which operates under its own distinct, merchant-compliant privacy terms.

4. Contact for Privacy Inquiries

For questions regarding our server logs, data retention procedures, or to request complete data deletion, please contact our compliance desk at sales@hostzin.example.com.
