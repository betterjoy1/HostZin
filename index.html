Common Lifetime Hosting Questions

Find answers to common questions about lifetime billing, resources, and service reliability.

What does "lifetime" mean?

Lifetime refers to the lifetime of the HostZin service (specifically, the LTHost service stack). We pool resources and invest in long-term infrastructure to sustain servers without requiring recurring hosting fees. Please see our Terms and Conditions for our standard service lifetime commitment.

Are there any recurring fees?

There are absolutely no recurring hosting fees for the resources included in your selected plan. However, third-party services such as custom domain registrations, premium theme or plugin licenses, or optional managed support add-ons are separate and may require renewal fees.

What is your uptime target?

We maintain a robust infrastructure and aim for an uptime target of 99.9% to ensure your web pages remain highly available and reliable for your global audience.

How do backups work?

We perform automated backups of all accounts once a week. These backups are stored securely off-server and can be restored easily via cPanel in the event of any data loss or emergency.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Yes! You can upgrade your lifetime hosting plan at any time by paying the difference in price between your current plan and the new tier. Upgrades are handled seamlessly without any downtime.

Is there a money-back guarantee?

Yes, we offer a 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee. If you are not completely satisfied with our performance or service, you can request a full refund within 7 days of your initial purchase.
